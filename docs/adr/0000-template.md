## Context
What forces are at play? What constraint, requirement or problem demands a decision? State facts and
measurements, not preferences.

## Decision
What we will do, stated in the active voice and specific enough to be falsifiable.

## Alternatives Considered
| Alternative | Why rejected |

| … | … |

Each alternative must be one a competent engineer would genuinely propose. Straw men are worse than
no alternatives at all.

## Consequences
**Positive** — what this buys us.
**Negative** — what it costs us. An ADR with no negative consequences has not been thought through.
**Risks** — what could make this decision wrong later, and what signal would tell us.
